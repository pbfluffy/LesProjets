### A: the goblin series (ids `gscrapper`, `garcher`, `gshaman`, `gking`)
All goblins: yellowish-to-olive green skin, huge pointed ears, big yellow eyes, sharp little teeth, scrappy junk-built gear (pot lids, spoons, rusty metal, leather scraps, bones, mushrooms), a cheeky and cunning look. A goblin is small: roughly half a hero's height (the boss is much bigger).
1. `gscrapper` Goblin Scrapper (swarm fighter, comes in groups): a small wiry goblin with a battered iron pot as a helmet, patched leather vest, a rusty cleaver, a torn loincloth, a wide mischievous grin. Idle: crouched ready, cleaver on the shoulder. Attack: a leaping slash with the cleaver, mouth wide.
2. `garcher` Goblin Archer (ranged): a lean goblin in a mossy green hood with a crude bow made of a bent branch and sinew, a quiver of bone-tipped arrows with feathers, squinting one eye. Idle: standing, bow lowered, an arrow nocked. Attack: the bow fully drawn, aiming left.
3. `gshaman` Goblin Shaman (heals the other goblins): a hunched old goblin wearing a big spotted mushroom cap as a hat, a necklace of bones and feathers, a gnarled staff topped with a glowing green crystal wrapped in vines, little swirling green wisps. Idle: leaning on the staff, wisps drifting. Attack: the staff raised high, the crystal flaring with green light.
4. `gking` Goblin King (stage boss, canvas about 640 px tall): a big pot-bellied goblin tyrant with a crown made of scrap metal, spoons and teeth, a cloak of patched rat fur, a belt of keys and trinkets, a gold tooth, and a huge notched cleaver used as a sceptre. Idle: standing proud, cleaver planted at his side, a smug grin. Attack: roaring and slamming the cleaver down.

### B: the orc series (ids `ogrunt`, `oberserker`, `obrute`, `owarlord`)
All orcs: grey-green skin, big lower tusks, heavy brows, muscular bodies, black topknots or shaved heads, war paint, crude black iron gear with leather straps and bone trophies. An orc is large: a bit taller than a hero (brute and warlord much bigger).
5. `ogrunt` Orc Grunt (armoured fighter): a stocky orc in a dented iron helmet and a single big iron pauldron, a studded leather harness, a crude single-headed axe, white war paint stripes. Idle: standing guard, axe resting on the shoulder. Attack: an overhead axe chop.
6. `oberserker` Orc Berserker (gets faster when hurt): a wild lean-muscled orc, bare scarred chest, red war paint, wild loose hair, a hand-axe in each hand, torn trousers, veins glowing slightly red. Idle: hunched, axes down, breathing hard. Attack: both axes swinging in a spinning slash.
7. `obrute` Orc Brute (heavy tank): a huge hulking orc in battered iron plates riveted to his chest and arms, a horned iron helm, chains over the shoulders, a giant slab-like iron hammer. Idle: standing heavy, hammer resting on the ground. Attack: the hammer slammed down in front.
8. `owarlord` Orc Warlord (stage boss, canvas about 640 px tall): a giant scarred orc warlord in spiked black-iron armour, a tall horned helm, a mantle of fur and skulls, a huge double-bladed war-axe, a tattered war banner on a pole on his back. Idle: standing tall, axe held upright. Attack: a huge axe swing with a war cry, mouth wide.
