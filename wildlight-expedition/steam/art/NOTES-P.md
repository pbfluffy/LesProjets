# Section P — Wildlight Expedition Steam art

- **Library capsule.** The 1024 × 1536 PNG master came from a reference-led portrait prompt for seven staggered heroes, Bren in front, a lit watchtower and an open top third. I composited the supplied logo across 85% of the final width. The title and faces fit the safe area. At 231 px wide the title stays readable, with Bren and several companions distinct. Tavi’s lower bow is partly hidden by Iona.
- **Library hero.** The 2172 × 724 PNG master came from a panoramic prompt for the six regions in order, eight heroes facing right and quiet mist at lower left. I regenerated twice to tighten the party to roughly x36–64% and give Steam’s logo more breathing room. The 1.77× Lanczos upscale shows slight softness in fine foliage and distant architecture at 100%. The major silhouettes remain clear.
- **Page background.** The 1672 × 941 PNG master came from a twilight valley prompt with a faint watchtower at the far right, restrained edge detail and a quiet text column. I regenerated the first pass to raise average brightness from 11.1% to 19.2%. The center is especially low contrast, and the bottom averages roughly RGB 24, 41, 59 near Steam’s #1b2838.

All three paintings came from the built-in image generator. I checked the final exports at full size, including 100% crops across the panoramic hero.
