# Section L — Wildlight Expedition logo

- Made a transparent 1280 × 554 PNG and kept the unresized 1983 × 793 magenta-backed PNG master.
- The built-in image generator responded well to a prompt for the exact title on two centered lines, with chunky golden-amber storybook serifs, a dark-brown outline, pale inner highlights, watchtower warmth, a small flame over the second i in Wildlight, a tiny four-point compass, and a flat magenta background. The game thumbnail, app icon, and region map guided the style.
- I regenerated the first draft to simplify the compass and open up the letterforms, then removed the magenta, unmixed the edge colors, and resized proportionally with high-quality bicubic filtering.
- Both words read at 231 × 100. The keyed edges looked clean at full size and at 231 px wide on #1b2330 and #f1ead8. The two lines still sit close together around the g descender.
