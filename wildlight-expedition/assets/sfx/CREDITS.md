# Sound effect credits

`pack.bin` / `index.json` hold short clips cut from the free sound packs below. Every pack is listed on OpenGameArt.org as **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**; no attribution is required, and the credit is given anyway.
The clips were trimmed to their useful part, normalised and converted (mono, 16-bit, 22.05 kHz) by `tools/build-sfx.js`; the groups they were sorted into are listed there. The original packs are not shipped.

|---|---|---|
| RPG Sound Pack | https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack | monster voices (beast, giant, ogre, shade, slime, beetle bites), cloth and armour, coins, clicks, spells, sword unsheathing, bubbles |
| Swishes Sound Pack | https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack | weapon swings |
| 80 CC0 RPG SFX | https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx | creature roars and cries, slime, spells, stones, wood, metal, coins, gems, locks, books, chains |
