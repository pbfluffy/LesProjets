# Music credits

The game's music is four recorded tracks, all listed on OpenGameArt.org with the license **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**: no attribution is required, and the credit is given anyway.
They were levelled to about -20 LUFS, the fight and boss songs were turned into seamless loops (the last seconds fade into the first seconds), and everything was saved as MP3 by `tools/build-music.js`. The original downloads are not shipped.

| File | Track | Author | Page | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `village1.mp3` | Medieval: The Bard's Tale (loop version) | RandomMind | https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-bards-tale | the village and map |
| `village2.mp3` | Medieval: The Old Tower Inn (loop version) | RandomMind | https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-old-tower-inn | the village and map (follows the first track) |
| `fight.mp3` | Medieval: Battle | RandomMind | https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-battle | normal fights |
| `boss.mp3` | Battle Theme A | cynicmusic | https://opengameart.org/content/battle-theme-a | boss fights |
